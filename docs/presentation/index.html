A symbolic evidence layer

A proposed evidence packet connects compact descriptions to inspectable sources.

Descriptive symbols retain observations and qualifications; they do not assert causation.

The proposed representation describes observations without turning them into causal claims. Typical reference behavior is not proof of health. Compression fidelity and diagnostic usefulness require separate evaluation.

Proposed investigation flow

The agent chooses the question. Deterministic tools produce the observations.

Finalize when evidence is sufficient or the investigation budget is reached.

This is the target architecture. Deterministic operations handle measurements, comparisons, and provenance. The agent decides what to investigate next. The loop stops when evidence is sufficient or the budget is reached, preserving unresolved alternatives.

What we add to OpenRCA

The proposed addition is the evidence layer and its operation contracts.

Both designs use iterative agents and tools. Performance gains remain unmeasured.

OpenRCA is the benchmark; RCA-agent is its baseline. Multimodal analysis, Python tools, adaptive follow-up, and execution bounds already exist upstream. Our proposed contribution is the evidence representation and its contracts, including reference qualification and a constrained operation interface. This is a comparison with the inspected baseline, not a claim of research novelty or measured superiority.
